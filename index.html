Overview & Biography

01 / BIOGRAPHY

Dr. Chi Zhang is currently a Faculty Member / Researcher at Yunnan Police College (since August 2025), Kunming, China. He received his Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Technology (with an integrated Master degree in Software Engineering) from Kunming University of Science and Technology (KUST) in June 2025.

My research focuses on blockchain-enabled trustworthy data sharing, privacy-preserving computation, and collaborative intelligence in distributed and multi-party environments. My work investigates how blockchain and distributed ledger technologies can support decentralized trust, data integrity, accountability, and secure data exchange among heterogeneous and potentially untrusted participants.

I integrate blockchain, game theory, multi-party computation (MPC), and collaborative computing to address challenges in trust establishment, incentive mechanisms, privacy preservation, and secure multi-party collaboration. These research interests provide a foundation for trustworthy data and intelligence sharing across organizational boundaries, with applications in IoT, edge intelligence, public safety, intelligent transportation, and emerging low-altitude ecosystems.

THRUST I

Blockchain & Trustworthy Data Sharing

Decentralized trust establishment, smart contract quality-driven incentive mechanisms, cross-chain anonymous authentication (Chain Bridge), and zero-trust/Byzantine fault-tolerant consensus protocols (Vssb-raft, RaBFT, SEKad).

THRUST II

Privacy-Preserving Computation & MPC

Atomic-level componentized Transformer inference with secure Multi-Party Computation (Secformer), homomorphically encrypted deep network inference, zero-knowledge mutual attestation (ZKSA), and probabilistic representations.

THRUST III

Collaborative Intelligence & Public Safety

Privacy-preserving collaborative model training for IoMT, knowledge-centric split & blockchained federated learning, collusion-based poisoning defense, and implicit association mining for smart anti-narcotics and low-altitude ecosystems.
