Overview & Biography

Dr. Chi Zhang is currently a lecture at Yunnan Police College (since August 2025). Prior to joining Yunnan Police College, he received his Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Technology (integrated Master–Ph.D. program, with Master's phase in Software Engineering) from Kunming University of Science and Technology (KUST) in June 2025.

His research focuses on blockchain-enabled trustworthy data sharing, privacy-preserving computation, and collaborative intelligence in distributed and multi-party environments. His work investigates how blockchain and distributed ledger technologies can support decentralized trust, data integrity, accountability, and secure data exchange among heterogeneous and potentially untrusted participants.

Methodologically, Dr. Zhang integrates blockchain protocols, game theory, secure multi-party computation (MPC), homomorphic encryption, and federated/split learning to address foundational challenges in trust establishment, incentive mechanisms, privacy preservation, and secure multi-party collaboration. These methodologies provide a theoretical and algorithmic foundation for trustworthy data and intelligence sharing across organizational boundaries, with domain applications in the Internet of Things (IoT), Internet of Medical Things (IoMT), edge intelligence, public safety, smart anti-narcotics, intelligent transportation, and emerging low-altitude ecosystems.

In addition to his research appointments, Dr. Zhang serves as an active peer reviewer for international journals published by IEEE IoTJ, IEEE TNSE,, Elsevier, Springer in the areas of information security, distributed ledger technologies, Internet of Things, and privacy-preserving machine learning.

Research Focus & Themes

My research investigates the theoretical foundations and system architectures required to establish verifiable trust, privacy guarantees, and incentive compatibility across distributed, multi-institutional networks.

Theme I

Blockchain & Decentralized Trust Infrastructure

Designing Byzantine fault-tolerant and zero-trust consensus protocols (VSSB-Raft, RaBFT), scalable P2P broadcast overlays (SEKad), cross-chain anonymous authentication and routing mechanisms (Chain Bridge), and quality-driven smart contract incentive mechanisms.

IEEE IoT-J · ACM TOSN · BCRA · Cluster Computing
Theme II

Privacy-Preserving Computation & MPC

Developing atomic-level componentized Transformer-like inference with secure multi-party computation (Secformer), noise budget management in homomorphically encrypted deep neural networks, oblivious transfer localization, and probabilistic representation co-design.

DCN · Electronics · Grant 2026J0800
Theme III

Collaborative Intelligence & Public Safety

Addressing Non-IID data, collusion-based poisoning attacks, and partial client participation in split and federated learning for IoMT and edge intelligence, alongside implicit association mining for smart anti-narcotics and public safety intelligence.

IEEE JBHI · IEEE TNSE · IEEE Network · Grant ZHJDNB-2025010
